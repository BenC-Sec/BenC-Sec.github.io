## New to CTI? Start Here.

I'm Ben, and this site is about the **practical implementation** of Cyber Threat
Intelligence. Plenty of guidance covers the theory; far less shows you how to
apply it day to day. That's the gap this site fills.

The Guides assume no CTI programme is in place — but by following them, you'll
effectively be building one. I point you to useful resources rather than
re-explaining textbook concepts: **this is where theory meets practice.**

### Read These in Order

A single beginner journey, from making the business case through to mapping
threat-actor TTPs:

1. **[Providing Business Value](/providing value/)** — win senior stakeholder
   support and start a CTI programme by speaking the business's language.
2. **[Initial Threat Landscape Report — Part 1: Language](/initial tlr lan/)** —
   frame cyber risk in business terms and standardise TTPs with MITRE ATT&CK.
3. **[Part 2: General Threat Environment](/initial tlr GTE/)** — begin
   identifying and assessing the threats facing the business.
4. **[Part 3: Converging on Specific Threats](/initial tlr CST/)** — narrow to
   the specific intrusion sets likely to target your country and sector.
5. **[Part 4: Threat Actor TTPs](/initial tlr RTA/)** — map each intrusion set's
   TTPs with MITRE ATT&CK Navigator into a single priority view.
